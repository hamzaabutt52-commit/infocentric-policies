Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Infocentric Video Publisher is a tool being developed to help its operator prepare short-form videos and upload them to TikTok as drafts.

1. Information We Process

The tool may process video files, TikTok account information made available through TikTok authorization, and authorization tokens needed to connect to TikTok.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate the video preparation and upload workflow, authorize access to TikTok, maintain the service, and troubleshoot technical issues.

3. TikTok and Third-Party Services

When TikTok integration is authorized, TikTok may process information and content according to its own policies and settings. Please review the TikTok Privacy Policy .

4. Storage and Security

Video files and integration credentials may be processed or stored in the operator's self-hosted n8n environment. Reasonable measures should be taken to protect credentials, restrict access, and prevent unauthorized use.

5. Data Retention and Contact

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to operate and maintain the video preparation and upload workflow. For privacy questions or requests about information handled by this tool, contact: jigersenterprises@gmail.com .

6. Your Choices

You can stop using the tool and revoke its TikTok access through the relevant TikTok account settings. Any stored information should be handled or deleted in accordance with applicable requirements and the operator's actual data-retention practices.

7. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as the tool develops. Changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
