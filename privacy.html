Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Infocentric Video Publisher is a tool being developed to help its operator prepare short-form videos and upload them to TikTok as drafts.

Information We Process

The tool may process video files, TikTok account information made available through TikTok authorization, and authorization tokens needed to connect to TikTok.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to operate the video preparation and upload workflow, authorize access to TikTok, and troubleshoot technical issues.

Third-Party Services

When you authorize TikTok integration, information and content are handled by TikTok according to its own policies and settings. Please review TikTok's Privacy Policy at TikTok Privacy Policy.

Storage and Security

Video files and integration credentials may be processed or stored in the operator's self-hosted n8n environment. Reasonable steps should be taken to protect credentials and restrict access.

Data Retention and Contact

Information should be retained only as long as necessary for the workflow. For privacy questions or requests about information handled by this tool, contact the service operator through the contact details provided on the official service website.

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as the service develops.
